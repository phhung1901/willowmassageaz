Licensed Estheticians

Sam

Samantha is an Arizona native and graduated from the Studio Academy of Beauty in 2018. Samantha is trained in European facial massage, speed body waxing, dermaplaning, and so much more! Book your appointment with Sam today and be ready to see younger, brighter, blemish-free skin!

“With brave wings, she flies.”
Mao

Mao moved to Arizona in 2019. She loves the scenery and winter here. In 2020, she graduated from Avalon School of Cosmetology. She is certified in dermaplaning, which is one of her favorite services, aside from waxing and facials. Francisco

Francisco (Chico) graduated from ASMT and specializes in sports, deep tissue, Cranial Sacral, and relaxation massage. His favorite focus areas are neck, shoulders, and hips with stretches. He takes pride in what he does because he loves his career. Taking care of others has always been his joy in life so one body at a time, he hopes to help as many people as he can, just like Superman!

Stephanie

Stephanie has been practicing Massage Therapy since February 2012 and has been with Willow since fall of 2014. With her unique style, Stephanie specializes in a handful of different modalities to perform a therapeutic massage for her clients. Creating an agenda with your requested needs, she knows no two services are ever alike.

Lucky enough for us here at Willow, she also performs the Young Living essential oil treatment Raindrop. Helping with deeper relaxation and Detox, the use of specific massage techniques down the spine help the essential oils go deeper into the body, creating a sympathetic response for the body to receive the best massage possible.

Also active outside of work, Stephanie enjoys yoga, AZ hiking, out of state travels, and home-made meals.

Sarah

Sarah graduated from Colorado Institute of Massage Therapy in 2016, beginning her career working with preconception, prenatal, and postpartum clients. Sarah’s focus in school was injury rehabilitation and women’s health. Sarah’s massage philosophy is when people feel more connected to their bodies, they are able to live more connected lives.

Hannah

Hannah graduated from the East Valley Institute of Technology (EVIT) in May of 2018. Her favorite modalities are deep tissue and pre-natal massage, though she always enjoys learning new techniques and applying them to her routine to create a unique session for all of her clients. Growing up, she was inspired by her father who was a massage therapist, so she has a lot of love for what she does. In addition to working in our spa, Hannah also spends time with clients at Quail Park and Eternal Spring Retirement Communities here in Gilbert.

Ambrosia

Ambrosia Myst Jones attended the Southwest Institute of Hearing Arts from 2018 – 2019, acquiring her massage license in October of 2019. She joined the Willow Massage team in November. She specializes in upper body thoracic outlet release, Swedish Sedona massage, and prenatal. She plans to continue her education and get a reflexology certification within the next few years.

Jasmine

Jasmine graduated from the South West Institute of Healing Arts in September 2020. She worked at a chiropractic office while she was a massage student and specializes in deep tissue, myofascial release, sports massage, and trigger point therapies. She also has a wide range of light pressure techniques such as lymphatic drainage. Jasmine is early in her career but has experienced working with clients with Parkinson’s Disease, cancer survivors and elderly clients. Though she was taught traditional therapeutic studies, she is open to doing energetic work and is beginning reiki.

Nicholas

Nicholas was born and raised in Arizona. Nicholas is unique in that he performs both facials and massages! He studied massage therapy at the East Valley Technical Institute of Technology and aesthetics at the Southwest Institute of Natural Aesthetics. Growing up, Nicholas always knew he had a passion and gift for skincare and healing the body naturally. He uses many different styles of massage: Swedish, trigger point, and stretching. His passion for skincare all started because of his own skincare journey; he knows how skin issues can really interfere with how you live your life and he hopes to help everyone feel comfortable in their own skin, to live life to the fullest every day.

Michele

Michele has been a therapist for 15 years. She specializes in rehabilitative techniques, blended with relaxation. Michele creates a safe, sacred space to facilitate healing for your mind, body, and soul. Michele also provides full body hot stone massages which will leave you blissfully, deeply relaxed.
