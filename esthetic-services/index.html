Only the best professional products such as PCA and Skin Script Products are used to ensure you are getting the highest level of service and best results possible. A relaxing facial massage, aromatherapy and cucumber eye treatments are just a few of the delightful extras we offer that will give you the best facial experience you’ve ever had. One treatment and I promise you’ll be booking that next appointment before you leave!

Add-Ons

Add-Ons to 60 Minute Facials: Add a dermaplane, microderm, waxing and lash or brow tinting to your facial for extra pampering and results driven services.
Dermaplaning
You’ve heard about it, now it’s time to try it for yourself! Because dermaplaning is a quick procedure with few to no adverse effects it has surged in popularity recently. It’s a first choice for patients who have excess vellus (peach fuzz) hair on the skin. Using a scalpel and a delicate touch, the provider simply abrades the surface of the skin using light feathering strokes. This is a great treatment if you are looking for a gentler way to achieve deep exfoliation and hair removal. If you are pregnant or nursing; this is an excellent option as well. Your skin will be smooth and product penetration is enhanced. Clients are sometimes concerned that the hair will grow back heavier and darker after dermaplaning. This is not the case. As long as the hair is superficial vellus hair, it will grow back at the same rate and texture as before the treatment.
Waxing

Say “bye bye” to unwanted hair in your lip area as well as shape those brow to enhance your beautiful eyes. Willow offers hard and soft wax options for even the most delicate, sensitive skin. A cooling gel and Green Tea lotion applied after treatment for that “extra” touch of pampering. Currently offering a free Bikini Wax to all first timers.

Microdermabrasion
This popular treatment is a noninvasive, skin resurfacing procedure that gently exfoliates or polishes away the top layer of dead skin cells using crystals and/or diamond tip wand. It can stimulate collagen growth, to reveal healthy new skin that looks and feels smoother. Fine lines, blemishes, dull skin and sun spots can be improved, especially with regular treatments. Products such as moisturizers, creams and lotions are more easily absorbed and therefore more effective after microdermabrasion treatment. This is a painless, low-risk skin treatment that you’ll fall in love with!
Lash Lift and Tint

We use the highly rated Lash Bomb products and techniques. This must be seen to be believed! An excellent, low cost, alternative to lash extensions. In just under an hour, your natural lashes will be permed, lifted and tinted for a beautiful, dramatic effect that lasts approximately 3-6 weeks. Minimal aftercare… just wake up and go- you’ll be the envy of your friends!

Lash and Brow Tinting
If you’ve ever taken a minute to shape, brush, or fill in your brows, then you’re no stranger to the idea that well-groomed brows can do wonders for your face. And one way to do that is with brow tinting. As we age, our lashes and brow hairs become lighter and less noticeable. Tinting looks more natural than pencils and think of the time you’ll save! Results vary but typically lasts 4-6 weeks Eyelash tinting is great for you ladies that have light colored lashes and want to save time and frustration of applying mascara. Looks and feels like your natural lashes and will enhance your eyes. Does not run when it gets wet and last approx. 4 weeks.
